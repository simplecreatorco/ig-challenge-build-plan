Day 2
Write your bio + build your highlights
▼
What to expectBig day. You're completing the entire first impression layer — bio and highlights. Your bio is your headline. Your highlights are your passive sales team. Together they handle the sales conversation before anyone DMs you.
Don't skip thisYour bio link goes directly to your offer — not a links page. Every line earns its place: value prop, identity qualifier, proof, one CTA. Nothing is decorative. And on highlights: order is not random. Skepticism gets neutralized before proof is presented.
  • ✓
    Use the Bio Template to write your 4-line bio — Line 1: value prop, Line 2: identity qualifier, Line 3: proof, Line 4: one CTA
  • ✓
    Update your bio link — link directly to your offer, not a links page
  • ✓
    Watch Lesson 6 — Highlights: Your Passive Sales Team
  • ✓
    Create Highlight 1 — Identity / Anti-Guru (kills the #1 objection in your niche upfront)
  • ✓
    Create Highlight 2 — Q&A (answer the hesitations so you never have to do it 1:1)
  • ✓
    Create Highlight 3 — Student Wins (prove it works for people like them)
  • ✓
    Create Highlight 4 — Freebie / Start Here (low-barrier funnel entry point)
  • ✓
    Design your highlight covers — consistent aesthetic, readable at small size on mobile
3
Day 3
Create your 3 pinned posts — the conversion trio
▼
What to expectYour pins take a cold visitor from curious to ready to act. Together they create a single narrative: I'm like you → here's what to do → here's what your life could look like. Today is a content creation day — block real time for it.
Don't skip thisBuild Pin 2 first — the Action Trigger. This is your highest-converting funnel entry point and where ManyChat lives. Write it with a keyword CTA (e.g. "comment FREE for the guide") even before ManyChat is set up. The keyword is the placeholder.
  • ✓
    Watch Lesson 7 — Pinned Posts: The Conversion Trio
  • ✓
    Create Pin 2 first — Action Trigger (educational carousel format, promises specific actionable value, ManyChat keyword CTA in the caption)
  • ✓
    Create Pin 1 — Relatable Proof (specific numbers + relatable identity + objection handled in one hook — makes the outcome feel real and achievable)
  • ✓
    Create Pin 3 — Emotional Proof (sells the lifestyle, not just the money — connects the outcome to something bigger than a number)
  • ✓
    Pin all three in order: Pin 1 → Pin 2 → Pin 3 from left to right on your profile grid
End of Phase 1 — Milestone ✦Your profile's entire first impression layer is complete. A cold stranger can land on your page and understand who you help, why you're credible, and exactly what to do next — without you saying a word. That's the system working.
Phase 2
Feed + ManyChat
4
Day 4
Set up your freebie + email funnel
▼
What to expectBefore you build your feed or turn on ManyChat, your freebie and email sequence need to be in place. ManyChat collects the email and delivers the freebie — the email sequence closes the sale. Get this built today.
  • ✓
    Create or finalize your freebie — it should solve one specific problem and deliver quick value
  • ✓
    Set up your email welcome sequence in your email platform (minimum 3 emails: deliver freebie → pitch offer → follow up)
  • ✓
    Make sure your email sequence pitches your low-ticket offer — ideally with an order bump
  • ✓
    Test your email opt-in flow from a second email address — does it deliver the freebie correctly?
5
Day 5
Build your feed — Qualifier + Objection Crusher posts
▼
What to expectYour feed is your sales page body copy — and every post in it has one job. Today you're creating the posts that call in your exact buyer and kill objections before they even come up. Aim for 2 of each type — 4 posts total. Every post needs three things: a Hook that stops the scroll, a Body that delivers on the hook's promise, and a Trigger (comment a keyword, DM me, click the link).
  • ✓
    Watch Lessons 8 + 9 — The Qualifier Post and The Objection Crusher Post
  • ✓
    Write + design 2 Qualifier posts — use hyper-specific identity language. Name the exact person: their situation, their time of night, what they're Googling. Make the right viewer feel seen.
  • ✓
    Write + design 2 Objection Crusher posts — lead with the objection AS the hook. Kill the #1 and #2 resistance points your buyer has before they even read the rest.
  • ✓
    Every caption needs a Trigger — a ManyChat keyword CTA, a DM prompt, or a link. No post ends without telling the viewer what to do next.
  • ✓
    Use the Post Lab to validate each caption against the framework before posting
6
Day 6
Build your feed — Proof + Curiosity Gap posts
▼
What to expectProof posts make the outcome feel real and achievable — be hyper-specific, never vague, never braggy. Curiosity Gap posts create incomplete information that demands resolution, forcing the watch and triggering the DM. Together these complete your foundation feed.
Don't skip thisEvery post type — not just Curiosity Gap — needs a Trigger in the caption. A ManyChat keyword, a DM prompt, or a link. Use a different keyword per offer so you can track which posts are driving which funnel entries. Write the keywords into your captions today even before ManyChat is configured.
  • ✓
    Watch Lessons 10 + 11 — The Proof Post and The Curiosity Gap Post
  • ✓
    Write + design 2 Proof posts — specific numbers, timelines, and relatable context. Never vague, never braggy. Make the outcome feel achievable for someone like your buyer.
  • ✓
    Write + design 2 Curiosity Gap posts — create incomplete information that forces the watch and demands resolution. End each with a ManyChat keyword CTA.
  • ✓
    Check your feed ratio: ~30% Objection Crusher, ~25% Proof, ~25% Curiosity Gap, ~20% Qualifier
  • ✓
    Curate your feed — hide anything that doesn't have a clear job. The feed is a living document, not a chronological archive. If you can't name what a post does, hide it.
7
Day 7
Set up ManyChat + test your full funnel
▼
What to expectManyChat is the loop that closes the sale. Today you're connecting everything — keyword trigger → DM sequence → offer pitch → email opt-in → freebie delivery. Every keyword across all your posts gets wired up. Test every step before moving on.
Don't skip thisManyChat must do two things: pitch the offer immediately in the DM sequence AND collect the email + deliver the freebie. The offer pitch in the DM comes first — before the email sequence starts. Test this from a second account or you won't catch what's broken.
  • ✓
    Watch Lesson 12 — The ManyChat Loop: How Your Funnel Closes the Sale. Then complete the full ManyChat mini-course bonus.
  • ✓
    Set up ManyChat using Rebecca's template — connect every keyword from your posts (one keyword per post/offer so you can track which content is driving entries)
  • ✓
    Build your DM sequence: immediate offer pitch first → email opt-in → freebie delivery
  • ✓
    Test the entire flow end-to-end from a second account — comment a keyword on one of your posts, go through every step of the DM sequence
  • ✓
    Confirm the email sequence triggers correctly and the freebie arrives in the test inbox
End of Phase 2 — Milestone ✦Halfway there — and the hardest part is done. Your profile is built, your feed is live, your funnel is connected and tested. The whole system exists. Phase 3 is making sure it's ready for paid traffic.
Phase 3
Pre-Launch Checks
8
Day 8
Full profile audit + cold traffic test
▼
What to expectBefore you spend a dollar on ads, you need to walk your profile like a cold stranger would. This is your final quality check. Anything that would lose a cold visitor gets fixed today.
Don't skip thisDo not run ads to a profile that hasn't passed the cold stranger test. Ads amplify what's already there — good or bad. A broken profile with paid traffic is just money going straight to Meta.
  • ✓
    Use the Profile Audit Checklist from your library — work through every point
  • ✓
    View your profile from a logged-out browser or a second account — what does a cold stranger see?
  • ✓
    Check: does your bio tell them who you help, prove it, and give a clear next step?
  • ✓
    Check: are your highlights in the right order and do they handle objections before showing proof?
  • ✓
    Check: do your 3 pins tell the full story — Pin 1: I'm like you → Pin 2: here's what to do → Pin 3: here's what your life could look like?
  • ✓
    Fix anything that didn't pass. Be honest — a cold stranger has no context.
9
Day 9
Set up your ad campaigns in Meta
▼
What to expectToday is setup day — you're building both ad campaigns in Meta Ads Manager, ready to launch tomorrow. You won't turn them on today. Get everything configured and reviewed first.
Don't skip thisYou need two separate campaigns: a Traffic ad that drives cold strangers to your profile, and an Engagement ad that amplifies your posts and triggers ManyChat comment keywords. Both start at $5–10/day. Watch Lesson 13 in full before you open Ads Manager.
  • ✓
    Watch Lesson 13 — Running Ads to Your Profile: The Simple Version
  • ✓
    Set up your Traffic ad in Meta Ads Manager — drives cold strangers to your profile. Use your best-performing organic content as the creative.
  • ✓
    Set up your Engagement ad — amplifies your posts and triggers ManyChat comment keywords on cold traffic. Use your highest-engagement post as the creative.
  • ✓
    Set daily budget to $5–10/day per campaign — do not start higher than this
  • ✓
    Review everything — targeting, creative, budget, destination URL — before submitting for review
  • ✓
    Submit both campaigns for Meta review — ads go live tomorrow on Day 10

Day 11
First look at your numbers
▼
What to expectYou've had ads running for about 24 hours. Your data is very early — don't read too deeply into it yet. Today is about getting familiar with what you're looking at, not making decisions.
A gentle reminderEarly data is noisy. If your ROAS looks low or your CTR isn't where you want it, that's completely normal at this stage. Give it until Day 13 before drawing any conclusions.
  • ✓
    Open Meta Ads Manager and note your Day 1 metrics: spend, reach, link clicks, CTR
  • ✓
    Check ManyChat — are keywords triggering? Are DMs coming in?
  • ✓
    Screenshot or note your Day 1 metrics somewhere you can reference them — early data is about establishing a baseline, not making decisions
  • ✓
    Do not make any campaign changes today — you don't have enough data yet

Day 12

What to expectToday you're watching the mid-funnel — not just ad clicks, but what happens after people land on your profile. Are they triggering ManyChat? Are they joining your email list? These are the signals that tell you your profile is converting.
  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓
    Check your sales dashboard — any purchases from the email sequence or DM offer pitch?

13
Day 13
Read your data — is it working?
▼
What to expectYou now have 3 full days of ad data. This is your first real read. You're not optimizing yet — you're looking at the headline numbers and understanding what they're telling you.
  • ✓
    Pull your 3-day totals: total spend, total revenue, ROAS
  • ✓
    Check your Link CTR — is it above 1%? If not, the ad creative may need attention
  • ✓
    Check your profile-to-DM conversion — are people who visit your profile triggering ManyChat?
  • ✓
    Pull your 3-day totals and note what the numbers are telling you — CTR, ROAS, ManyChat entries, sales
  • ✓
    If ROAS is at 1.4 or above — your system is working. Hold and observe through Day 14.
  • ✓
    If ROAS is below 1.0 — don't panic. Start with CVR, then CTR, then AOV to understand where the breakdown is
